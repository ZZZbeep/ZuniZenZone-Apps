Privacy Policy

Zuni's ZenZone Apps — KonshusKreashunCo

Thank you for using Zuni's ZenZone. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how KonshusKreashunCo ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our mobile applications: Zuni's ZenZone: Celestial, Zuni's ZenZone: Frequency, and Zuni's ZenZone: Aligned (collectively, the "Apps").

1. Information We Collect

Our Apps are designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store or share any personally identifiable information. We do not require you to create an account or log in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or any personal contact information. We do not track your location. We do not share your data with third parties.

Some features (such as the practice streak tracker in Zuni's ZenZone: Aligned) store data locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us.

2. Subscription & Payments

If you choose to subscribe to Zuni's ZenZone membership, all payment processing is handled securely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment information directly.

3. Third Party Services

Our Apps use Google Fonts for typography. No personal data is transmitted through this service beyond standard web request information.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this document.
